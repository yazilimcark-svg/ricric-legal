Ricric Account Deletion

Last updated: September 21, 2026

How to Delete Your Ricric Account

Ricric users can delete their account through the account deletion function available within the Ricric application.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When you request account deletion, your account and associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where applicable, subject to information that may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or resolve disputes.

User Content

Content associated with your account may also be deleted or removed as part of the account deletion process, subject to applicable legal or security requirements.

Contact

If you have difficulty deleting your account through the application, please contact the Ricric support team using the contact information provided in the application.
