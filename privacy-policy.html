Ricric Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Ricric respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the Ricric application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Ricric, we may collect:

  • Email address or information provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Profile information and profile photo.
  • Photos and other content that you choose to upload.
  • Location information when you enable and use location features.
  • Device and technical information necessary for the operation and security of the application.
  • Information necessary to provide authentication, account management and application functionality.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your Ricric account.
  • Provide and maintain Ricric features.
  • Display your profile and content to other users where applicable.
  • Provide location-based features when enabled.
  • Protect users and maintain application security.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and violations of our rules.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Photos and User Content

Ricric allows users to upload photos and other content. You are responsible for the content you upload. Content that violates Ricric rules may be removed. Users can report inappropriate content and block other users.

4. Location Information

If you grant permission, Ricric may use your device location to provide location-related features. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

5. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Ricric uses Firebase services to provide certain application functions, including authentication, data storage and related technical services. Information processed by these services is handled according to the applicable service providers' policies and our instructions.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes. When an account is deleted, information is deleted or anonymized where applicable, subject to legitimate legal or security requirements.

8. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Ricric account. Account deletion and related information are handled according to the Ricric account deletion process.

9. Children's Privacy

Ricric is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation is strictly prohibited. Users can report inappropriate content and block other users.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The updated version will be published on this page with a new update date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the Ricric support team through the contact information provided in the application.
